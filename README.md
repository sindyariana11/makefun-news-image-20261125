A ledger is a book that is not allowed to lie. Every entry dated,
every figure sourced, every claim entered as a claim. This page
keeps Tuesday 25 November 2026 in that book: thirty-seven stories,
nine regions, one day — and the books balance.

The three largest entries stand at the head of the page. NATO's
early-warning deployment to Poland, posted after Russian missiles
landed on Polish soil during the attack on Ukraine, with
Chancellor Merz's promise of complete support entered beside it.
Baghdad's order to seize the Kurdish oilfields, with President
Barzani's answer — Kurdistan will not submit — posted in the same
hand. And Gaza, where drone strikes killed seven: a Hamas security
chief, three policemen, and three siblings, one of them a child.
The ledger does not round grief down.

Below them, the day's ordinary postings, each with its scene drawn
and its certainty marked. A claim from Hormuz — Iran says three
tankers were mined — is entered under CLAIMED, in those words,
because the book distinguishes what is known from what is said.
A count of Russian war dead past 700,000. Brussels' new sanctions.
Fifty Boeing jets on Xiamen's order book. A car bomb in Colombia
and a title restored in Senegal. Twelve coral species written back
into the living columns of the Great Barrier Reef. Madrid's five
goals. Nvidia's Monday — entered under Monday Close, the date it
belongs to, at +31.5%. And the PCE report, due this day and not
yet posted: the ledger leaves its line open and prints no figure
in its place.

The bookkeeping furniture is part of the audit. A number strip
carries the day's eight heaviest figures, each tagged with the
card it came from, so any figure can be traced to its entry. A
date strip sets the day among its neighbours — the rationing that
began on the 22nd, Monday's close, today's report, a 2027 vote —
so nothing is mistaken for belonging to Tuesday that does not.
Region counts are posted only for stories actually entered. The
map is pinned by card number. The DATE CHECK page lists four
items struck out of the day: a Mercury story dated 23 November,
climate talks from earlier in the month, a Liverpool result from
an earlier round, and a game launch with no release today.

How it was kept: `compose_a.py` and `compose_b.py` wrote the day
two ways; the duel between them is recorded in the build notes,
and the A hand — charcoal, signal orange, the date set largest on
the page — is the one that stands in `entry.png`. `main.py`
carries the finished page to `/output/entry.png` unchanged.

Posted from the wires of RFE/RL, Al Jazeera, DW, Starlit Daily and
Forbes. The drawings are original to this ledger.
